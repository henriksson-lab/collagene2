are MIT licensed. This is not an open-data license grant from the named suppliers.
Scientific facts, product names and citations identify their sources; no supplier
endorsement, universal compatibility or molecular modification measurement is implied.

The current edition's `sources` records retain exact primary URLs, review dates,
source/version descriptions, qualified snapshot availability and assertion hashes.
These hashes bind our curated assertions. They do not authenticate a vendor's
website or establish scientific accuracy. Vendor documents, trademarks and other
protected material retain their own rights and are not sublicensed by our MIT file.

reviewed 2026-11-28. No HTML, PDF, image, website table layout, vendor prose or
scientific implementation is included. Newly reviewed sources have no raw snapshot
capture; no upstream byte hash is fabricated. The INV110 source is manufacturer
manual MAN0000103 revision A.0; its raw PDF is not redistributed here.
