# Overlap-extension PCR followed by restriction digestion

The preparation uses one original source for two separate PCRs, reciprocal
extension of their declared terminal homology, and a final outer PCR. The final
amplicon then enters a pinned catalog digest. The operator chooses a digest
fragment and may declare end repair before the normal assembly treatment and
joining steps. All six oligos, explicit search limits, catalog identity, fragment
number and original material origin belong to the retained request.

The inspector must reconstruct these steps from the original material. An
imported DNA sequence or a displayed intermediate does not establish the physical
substrate. Saved material requires fresh replay of its exact originating graph.
The inspector has no intended-target argument and cannot choose a geometry by
matching desired DNA. Competing source-PCR or outer-PCR geometries withhold a
sole amplicon; incomplete reciprocal-extension enumeration remains conditional.
Digest inspection uses the actual freshly established duplex, including its
outer-PCR termini, rather than scanning the original template or trusting
serialized physical metadata.

Choosing a fragment changes only its requested fragment number. Assembly
verification reconstructs preparation and digestion again, then checks the
assembled product against the separately frozen intended construct. Complete
cleavage, PCR amplification, enzyme/reagent compatibility and laboratory success
remain stated model assumptions rather than experimental observations.

Replacing an existing PCR preparation with suggested overlap-extension oligos
preserves its downstream pinned digest. Pins, fragment numbers, repair, source
origins and target declarations cannot be changed by the suggestion application.
The new upstream product may require the user to inspect and choose a different
fragment; the proposal does not infer that choice. Manual starter-enzyme digests
must first be converted to an explicit catalog selection.

Draft version9 identifies the new compound selector. Existing unfinished six-oligo
editors retain their version8 capability floor. Historical operations and their
scientific interpretation are unchanged; this preparation compiles existing
operations with explicit source links. Worker84 gates this new preparation and
target-free inspector; candidate18, native-plan16 and science22 remain unchanged.
The MCP inspector uses the same reconstruction as the web picker.

Focused native validation passes, including actual digest products, saved origins,
conditional selection, competing PCR geometries, old-format rejection, stale
settings and named-design retention. Native compilation and the final WASM
check pass. The public preview still uses the earlier published viewer
snapshot; no browser checks were run. Browser acceptance remains deferred until
the complete in-scope implementation is finished.
