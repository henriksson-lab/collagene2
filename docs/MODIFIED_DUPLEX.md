# Tracked modifications and checked digestion

`core::modified_duplex` wraps the existing `chemistry::Duplex` without changing
its serialized layout. The wrapper retains positional N6-methyladenine (`N6A`)
and 5-methylcytosine (`C5C`) state on each actual strand stored 5′→3′. Its engine
is `modified-duplex-1`; the checked profile is
`positional-neb-starter-2026-11-24-v1`. This is a bounded physical-state model
under explicit assumptions, not measured methylation or experimental success.

## State and source assumptions

Each strand has a default state per chemical and sparse half-open spans. States
are unknown, unmodified or modified. Defaults and unmodified/unknown spans apply
only to chemically eligible A or C calls. A modified span must contain only the
corresponding base. Overlapping spans for the same chemical, invalid order,
out-of-strand coordinates and incompatible modified bases are rejected. Nothing
is inferred from imported metadata.

`from_unknown_duplex` establishes unknown state at all eligible calls. It does
not allocate a dense per-base array or reduce legacy ten-million-base strand
capacity. `from_input` requires an exact immutable intact-duplex input and
explicit `GenerationAssumptions { dam, dcm, cpg }`, bound to its original source
revision. This source-generation model explicitly scopes modifications to those
three mechanisms. Outside their **original** motifs, eligible calls are assumed
unmodified. A fully modified category marks both strand targets; unknown or
hemi/mixed categories leave their potential targets unknown. No fractional or
arbitrarily oriented hemi state is invented. Other modification mechanisms are
excluded under this declared generation assumption, not experimentally ruled
out. Imported physical-intermediate metadata cannot establish such an input.

Motifs are expanded exactly once on the original source. Retained material carries its recorded source
revision and assumptions through descendant copying operations. Fresh PCR
products have newly synthesized unmodified state; the native graph retains the
preceding source-generation assumptions. In particular, cutting
away part of a Dam motif does not erase a retained methyladenine. Reapplying
source generation to an already generated product is forbidden by plan
reconstruction; it is not a descendant-state reset mechanism.

## Physical propagation

All retained-state mappings address actual strand indices, not annotation
coordinates or the projected top-strand molecule. Unknown state is copied as
unknown. Newly synthesized calls are explicitly unmodified under the existing
standard-nucleotide/unmodified-oligo assumptions.

* Reverse swaps the two stored strands and their ledgers. Their actual 5′→3′
  sequence indices are preserved; top-reference geometry changes separately.
* Digest slices each actual strand at its own cut boundaries, with circular
  wrap mapping. The public fragment mapper checks source/product bases and
  coordinate consistency. Cut-free circular survivors remain circular.
* Ligation maps top pieces in supplied order and bottom pieces in reverse
  piece order. Circular bottom-strand realignment rotates its ledger too.
  Newly formed methylase motifs receive no automatic modification.
* End treatment changes terminal chemistry only.
* End repair consumes the existing `StrandMapping` evidence: retained residues
  keep their states, removed residues disappear, and fill-in residues are new
  unmodified calls. Template methylation is not copied into synthesized bases.
* Tailing retains source calls and marks the explicit new 3′ residues
  unmodified. Existing accessible-OH requirements remain enforced.
* Ideal PCR resets the newly synthesized product to unmodified; it does not
  model retained parental molecules or reaction carryover. Fresh oligo
  annealing uses the explicit standard-unmodified-oligo assumption. Sequence
  overlap operations without a supported physical strand mapping do not gain
  a physical modification state.

The legacy unconditional digest preserves positional state while retaining its
old cleavage assumptions. Its behavior is not silently promoted to checked
cleavage. Plan verification discloses legacy cleavage of declared modified or
unresolved sources, instead of hiding a contradiction behind a matching DNA
sequence.

## Checked positional profile

The profile hash binds the positional rules and the factual methylation
catalog. Pin it explicitly in checked operations. Recognition sites are scanned
on both orientations with circular context, but chemical states are read from
the inherited ledgers, never regenerated by the scan. A recognition span must
be fully paired on both actual strands. Single-stranded overhang matches and
unavailable cut substrate cannot establish a digest product.

Published factual sources are [NEB's methylation guide](https://www.neb.com/en/tools-and-resources/selection-charts/dam-dcm-and-cpg-methylation)
and its [XbaI context FAQ](https://www.neb.com/faqs/is-xbai-affected-by-methylation).
The exact supplier/formulation limitations and conflicting generic BsaI facts
remain documented in [METHYLATION.md](METHYLATION.md).

Supported definite blocking cases are full internal CpG modification on both
strands throughout a NotI or SmaI site, and actual full Dam modification in a
published intact overlapping XbaI context. Partial/hemi CpG subsets are not
promoted to those full-substrate rules. A known blocking case retains the site
uncut; if all circular sites are blocked, the intact circular molecule survives.

A site can pass the scoped methylation check when every relevant eligible call
is unmodified, or an actual chemical position is covered by a published
insensitive methylase context. This checks the existing chemical state and
applicable context; it does not regenerate source state. Modified or unknown
positions without such a complete rule remain unknown. In particular, an
inherited XbaI methyladenine after its original `GATC` motif breaks cannot become
permitted merely because the current source lacks `GATC`. Some-combination
rules, unsupported generic formulations and unresolved partial states remain
unknown. `Impaired` is represented explicitly and never interpreted as complete
cleavage or complete blocking.

Any unknown/impaired site, incomplete scan or unsupported physical geometry
withholds the fully determined fragment set. The engine does not pick a partial
digest pattern. Permitted cuts use the same validated two-strand geometry as
legacy digestion; blocked cuts are omitted. Shared boundaries and conflicting
strand cuts retain existing geometry checks.

## Bounds, provenance and authority

Tracked structures use bounded serialization (32 MiB), at most 16,384 spans per
strand and 1,000 generation records. Copying and synthesis operate on sparse
intervals. Source generation and checked scanning retain the one-million-base
profile limit, at most 4,096 sites and bounded shared recognition/context work;
legacy unknown-state wrapping/mapping retains the original larger strand limit.
Tracked digest output also has an aggregate byte budget, including repeated
provenance. Bounds fail explicitly or return an incomplete checked report with
no claimed complete product set.

`CheckedDigestReport::validate` checks bounded report shape;
`validate_bindings_for` additionally binds the exact tracked source, options and
current engine/profile. Neither reconstructs science from imported reports.
`replay_checked` freshly recomputes and requires exact equality. Plan products
retain only descriptive bounded modification summaries and source-generation
provenance in molecule metadata. Full strand state is reconstructed from the
immutable native graph. A portable summary never restores physical authority.

This milestone is part of F21. Formulation-specific catalog expansion, exact
additional positional susceptibility predicates, and curated reaction-condition
compatibility with supplier/version provenance remain outstanding. Passing this
modification check does not establish buffer compatibility, temperature,
kinetics, star activity, laboratory yield or experimental completion.
