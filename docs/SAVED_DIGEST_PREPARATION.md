# Saved digest material in cloning preparations

`assembly_recipe::Preparation::FromDigest { origin, preparation }` explicitly reuses an exact saved digest-fragment event. The appended variant leaves historical preparation tags unchanged. Its `RunPreparation` child is the same flat vocabulary used by `FromRun`: unchanged material, exact/mismatch PCR, catalog or ordinary digest, PCR followed by digest, or the four-operation overlap-extension PCR preparation (optionally followed by catalog digest and repair).

The compiler emits an input-only `ReconstructDigestFragment` before the child operations. The child consumes that reconstructed step; it cannot bypass the physical origin through a bare sequence input. Prepared-row ownership includes the reconstruction, forked overlap-extension PCR graph, and optional digest/repair. Existing maximums remain seven operations per preparation, eight with ligation treatment, and nine with tailing treatment. All source capsules, including the outer saved-digest wrapper and source snapshot, share the native aggregate material budget before copies.

The verifier replays the retained original native recipe and its independent target, then the exact pinned catalog digest and fragment selection. A digest does not acquire an invented target. The subsequent new assembly has its own independently bound target. Captured run inputs must name the exact derived product event, not merely an identical DNA revision. Imported duplex summaries, chemistry labels, historical evidence and names do not create fresh tracked material.

Historical physical evidence binds the complete digest capsule and input event. Native replay preserves original source modifications and incomplete origin findings; a new PCR subsequently follows the ordinary native PCR model rather than inheriting parental methylation. Documentary evidence remains distinct from current physical authority.

Primer orders contain only explicitly declared new PCR/annealing steps. The reconstructed origin's primers are retained as provenance but are excluded from new orders. PDF instructions describe reconstruction, original source/product events, pinned fragment selection and inherited uncertainty as material reuse rather than new execution of historical reactions.

`core/tests/run_digest_preparation.rs` covers a saved PCR cut fragment followed by fresh PCR and physical overlap assembly, a reusable but search-incomplete physical circular origin propagated through the same downstream workflow, exact-event/revision refusal, and preservation of source modifications at reconstruction. The fixtures bind literal expected DNA independently, record real native runs and replay saved archives; they do not reconstruct authority from serialized strand summaries.
