# Original MoClo 2011 Level-0 module paths

Source: Weber E, Engler C, Gruetzner R, Werner S, Marillonnet S (2011),
*A Modular Cloning System for Standardized Assembly of Multigene Constructs*,
PLoS ONE 6(2):e16765,
[doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0016765](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0016765).

The implementation and independently authored factual profile are MIT. The
upstream article remains under its own attribution license. No upstream prose,
figure, plasmid sequence collection, cloning application data or application code
is redistributed. `source-provenance.json` records exact bounded publisher XML
and Figure1 PNG captures, reviewed 2026-11-28. The PNG was visually inspected.

Figure1B/C specifies the role/fusion pairs. Its labels are reference-strand
5′→3′ fusion sequences, including the right junction. They are not
reverse-complemented physical-end labels. This profile accepts one complete
four-role cytosolic or five-role signal-peptide module path. It requires BsaI/BpiI
body-site absence for the scoped original entry/release policy; Esp3I screening
is advisory for later hierarchical reuse. Recognition facts were crosschecked
against the official [NEB isoschizomer table](https://www.neb.com/en-de/tools-and-resources/selection-charts/isoschizomers)
and exact pinned catalogue geometries. Vendor prose/assets are not redistributed.

Bodies are supplied explicitly; format additions are empty. No bases are inserted
for the ATG or GGT fusion overlaps, and no reading frame or protein processing is
certified. Combined modules, higher levels, vector compliance, functionality,
physical assembly and success are outside this profile. Those limits distinguish
the implemented local syntax from a claim of complete MoClo-toolkit compliance.
